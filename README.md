# TaxiGo GTFS - Minibus Taxi Transit Data
Transit data of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia — based on OpenStreetMap and Traffic Management Authority sources.
---

This repository hosts an open (but early and experimental) dataset of minibus routes in Addis Ababa.

## ⚠️ The GTFS dataset is not yet available — coming soon!

## 🙌 Credits
This project builds on publicly available data from OpenStreetMap and the Addis Ababa Traffic Management Authority.
A significant portion of the OSM data was contributed by the local mapping community, including efforts by the AddisMapTransit.com team. Their work has greatly improved the coverage and quality of transport data in Addis Ababa, and we appreciate their contributions to the ecosystem.
